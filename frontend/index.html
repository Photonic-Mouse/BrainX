Project Architecture & Scientific Objectives

Objective

The BrainX project investigates automated classification of brain MRI scans into four dementia-related categories using transfer learning with ResNet18 and explainability with Grad-CAM, comparing three image preprocessing paradigms.

1. Mild Demented

Recognizable cortical thinning and ventricular expansion with moderate functional cognitive impairment.

2. Moderate Demented

Severe ventricular enlargement, prominent sulcal widening, and pronounced cerebral volume loss.

3. Non Demented

Normal age-matched cerebral architecture with preserved cortical thickness and standard ventricular dimensions.

4. Very Mild Demented

Incipient subtle atrophy corresponding to early Mild Cognitive Impairment (MCI).

Methodology & Training Details

  • Dataset: 6,400 axial MRI scans strictly partitioned into 70% Train ($4,480$), 15% Validation ($960$), and 15% Held-out Test ($960$).
  • Backbone: ResNet18 pretrained on ImageNet, fine-tuned with Adam optimizer ($\text{lr} = 10^{-4}$), batch size 16, and class-weighted cross-entropy loss.
  • Explainability Engine: Gradient-weighted Class Activation Mapping (Grad-CAM) targeting Layer 4 residual convolutional feature maps.
